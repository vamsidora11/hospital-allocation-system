## Phase 1 — Models & schema

- `app/models/domain.py`: Pydantic models from plan Section 3 (`PatientInfo`, `RouterOutput`,
  `PolicyCitation`, `AgentResult`, `ApprovalRecord`, `AuditEntry`, `WorkflowState`).
- `app/models/orm.py`: SQLAlchemy tables from plan Section 4 (`requests`, `patients`, `beds`,
  `staff`, `agent_results`, `policy_citations`, `approvals`, `audit_log`, `policy_documents`,
  `policy_chunks`). `policy_chunks.chroma_id` references the Chroma collection instead of a
  pgvector column, keeping the schema portable to the SQLite dev/test fallback.
- `app/db.py`: engine/session factory, SQLite-aware (`check_same_thread` only set for SQLite).
- Alembic wired up (`alembic.ini`, `alembic/env.py`, `alembic/script.py.mako`) and one
  autogenerated migration (`4c192a272822_initial_schema.py`). Added a hand-written step that
  revokes `UPDATE`/`DELETE` on `audit_log` for `PUBLIC`, gated on `dialect.name == "postgresql"`
  since SQLite has no row-level GRANT model — the append-only guarantee only applies in Postgres.
- `app/seed.py`: seeds synthetic beds (31: 6 ICU / 20 general / 5 isolation), staff (8, mixed
  roles/specialties/shifts), and the 5 policy document rows (pointing at `data/policies/*.md`,
  content itself lands in Phase 2).

**Verification performed:**

  present (`agent_results, approvals, audit_log, beds, patients, policy_chunks,
  policy_citations, policy_documents, requests, staff`).
- `py -3 -m app.seed` — ran cleanly, seeded 31 beds / 8 staff / 5 policy documents, idempotent
  (checks `count() == 0` before inserting).
- Imported and instantiated `PatientInfo` (and the rest of `app/models/domain.py`) to confirm
