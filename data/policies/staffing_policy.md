# Staff Scheduling Policy

Policy ID: STAFF-SCHED-01
Effective date: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nursing Operations

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines nurse-to-patient ratio requirements, specialist matching rules, and escalation
procedures for staffing gaps across bed categories.

## 2. Shift structure

Shifts run in three eight-hour blocks (day, evening, night). Ratio compliance is evaluated per
shift, not as a daily average, since a compliant daily average can still mask a non-compliant
individual shift.

## 3. Roles and credentials

Nursing staff are credentialed in one or more of: general ward care, ICU care, respiratory and
infectious care, and cardiac care. A credential must be current and on-shift to count toward
ratio or specialist-matching compliance; a credentialed nurse who is off-shift does not satisfy
this policy.

## 4. General ratio requirements

### 4.1 General ward ratios

General Ward beds require a maximum 1:4 nurse-to-patient ratio per active shift.

### 4.2 Isolation ward ratios

Isolation Room beds require a maximum 1:3 nurse-to-patient ratio per active shift, reflecting the
additional donning/doffing and monitoring burden of isolation care.

## 5. ICU and specialist staffing

### 5.1 ICU nurse ratios

ICU beds require a maximum 1:2 nurse-to-patient ratio per active shift. This ratio may not be
relaxed to accommodate volume; if it cannot be met, follow the escalation procedure in Section 6.0
rather than admitting beyond capacity.

### 5.2 Specialist availability windows

Specialist coverage (cardiac, respiratory, trauma) is scheduled in overlapping windows to avoid
gaps at shift changeover. A specialist is considered available only within their scheduled window.

### 5.3 Specialist matching

Cases with a confirmed specialty diagnosis are matched to an on-shift specialist before general
staff. For example, a confirmed acute myocardial infarction is matched to a cardiac-credentialed
nurse rather than a general ward nurse, even if doing so requires a shift reassignment.

### 5.4 Isolation staffing cross-reference

Isolation-room patients additionally require a nurse credentialed in respiratory or infectious
care for the duration of stay, per Section 2.2 of the Infection Control Standards. This
requirement applies on top of, not instead of, the ratio in Section 4.2.

## 6.0 Coverage shortfalls

When credentialed coverage is unavailable for the current shift — whether a ratio shortfall or a
missing specialist match — escalate to human review before assignment. Do not assign a
non-credentialed nurse to a role requiring a specific credential, and do not silently exceed a
ratio limit; both require documented reviewer sign-off logged against the affected shift.

## 7. Audit and reporting

Ratio compliance and specialist-match outcomes are logged per shift and reviewed weekly by Nursing
Operations. Repeated shortfalls in the same unit trigger a staffing-model review independent of any
individual patient decision.
