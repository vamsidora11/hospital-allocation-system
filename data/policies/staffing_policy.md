# Staff Scheduling Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## 5.1 ICU nurse ratios

ICU beds require a maximum 1:2 nurse-to-patient ratio per active shift. Any ICU bed assignment
must be validated against current shift staffing before being finalized; if the ratio cannot be
maintained with on-shift nurses, the assignment must be flagged for human review rather than
silently approved.

## 5.3 Specialist matching

Cases with a confirmed specialty diagnosis are matched to an on-shift specialist before general
staff. Cardiac presentations should be matched to a cardiac-credentialed nurse or physician when
one is on shift; similarly for other diagnosis categories with a clear specialty mapping.
Specialist matching is preferred but not mandatory when no specialist is on shift — in that case,
fall back to general staff and note the gap.

## 6.0 Coverage shortfalls

When credentialed coverage is unavailable for the current shift, escalate to human review before
assignment. This applies to any staffing decision — ICU ratio, isolation credentialing, or
specialist matching — where the policy-required coverage cannot be confirmed against the current
roster. A staffing recommendation must never be finalized silently in the presence of a known
coverage gap.
