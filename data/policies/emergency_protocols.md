# Emergency Protocols

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## 1.1 Priority-1 criteria

Cardiac, respiratory, or trauma presentations with unstable vitals are classified Priority 1 and
accepted regardless of current department load. Priority 1 classification overrides diversion
thresholds and capacity-based routing — these patients are never diverted or transferred solely
for load reasons.

## 2.3 Diversion thresholds

Minor-acuity cases may be diverted to urgent care when emergency department load exceeds
capacity thresholds. Diversion is only appropriate for minor severity with stable vitals and no
Priority 1 indicators. Diversion decisions must record the specific load metric that triggered
them.

## 3.0 Regional transfer

Cases requiring specialist capability unavailable on-site are transferred to the designated
regional facility. Transfer applies regardless of severity when the required specialist
capability (e.g. a specific surgical or interventional service) is not available at the current
site, and should be distinguished from diversion, which is a capacity decision rather than a
capability decision.
