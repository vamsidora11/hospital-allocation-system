# Infection Control Standards

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## 1.3 Respiratory isolation

Respiratory distress with suspected infectious origin requires a negative-pressure isolation
room where available. This applies in addition to, not instead of, the general isolation
criteria in the bed allocation policy — a respiratory-infectious presentation should be routed to
negative-pressure isolation specifically rather than a standard isolation room whenever one is
available.

## 2.2 Isolation staffing

Isolation-room patients require a nurse credentialed in respiratory or infectious care for the
duration of stay. This credentialing requirement is independent of, and in addition to, any
specialty matching from the staffing policy — an isolation assignment without confirmed
infectious-care credentialing on shift must be escalated per the staffing policy's coverage
shortfall rule.
